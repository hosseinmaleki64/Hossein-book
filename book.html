In the fast-paced world of financial markets, success requires more than technical expertise — it calls for a deep understanding of human behavior and disciplined decision-making. “The Art of Trading” bridges the gap between traditional and behavioral finance, exploring how psychology, risk perception, and emotion shape market performance. This comprehensive guide empowers readers to master technical and fundamental analysis while developing a mindset suited for long-term success in trading and investing.
